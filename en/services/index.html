What we offer

Specialized services for lasting change

Every program we offer is evidence-based, certified, and delivered with a human-centered approach. We serve individuals across Dallas, Denton, Collin, Tarrant, and Ellis Counties.

  • DWI Basic Education

    Educational program designed to inform individuals about the risks and consequences of driving while impaired.

  • DWI Intervention

    Early intervention program for individuals convicted of DWI to promote awareness and responsible decision-making.

  • Substance Abuse Evaluation

    Professional assessment to determine the nature and extent of substance use and recommend appropriate treatment.

  • Relapse Prevention

    Strategies and tools to help individuals identify triggers and maintain long-term recovery.

  • Drug Offender Program

    Specialized program for individuals with drug-related offenses to support accountability and behavioral change.

  • Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

    Structured treatment program offering intensive support while allowing individuals to maintain daily responsibilities.

  • Supportive Outpatient Program (SOP)

    Ongoing support and counseling for individuals in recovery to promote stability and continued progress.

  • Anger Management Program

    Tools and techniques to help individuals understand and manage anger in healthy ways.

  • Trauma/PTSD Program

    Specialized therapy to help individuals heal from trauma and manage symptoms of PTSD.

  • EMDR Therapy

    Evidence-based therapy that helps process traumatic memories and reduce emotional distress.

  • MRT Program

    Moral Reconation Therapy to improve decision-making and reduce criminal thinking.

  • Thinking for a Change Program

    Cognitive-behavioral program designed to help individuals change thinking patterns and behaviors.

  • Battering Intervention Program

    Program for individuals who have used abusive behavior to promote accountability and non-violence.

  • Theft Intervention

    Focused on addressing the underlying issues related to theft and promoting positive choices.

  • WEST Program

    Weekend Employment and Support Team program to reduce recidivism through employment and support.

  • Life Skills Program

    Build essential life skills to improve personal growth, communication, and problem-solving.

  • Parenting Skills Course

    Support and education for parents to build stronger relationships and effective parenting skills.

  • Pre-Trial Services

    Assistance and supervision for individuals during the pre-trial process.

  • Parole / Probation Support

    Support and guidance for individuals on parole or probation to help meet requirements and achieve success.
