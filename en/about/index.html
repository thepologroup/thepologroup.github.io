About Our Company

The Polo Group, LLC was founded by Lilliam Polo on September 20, 2010. Ms. Polo has been a cornerstone in the industry for more than 30 years, sharing her expertise and honing her skills to create a sanctuary for healing.

Having obtained her advanced degree from the University of Puerto Rico, her journey led her to further academic excellence at UNT, UTD, and ICDS. Today, we stand as a beacon for those seeking a modern, empathetic pathway back to their best selves.
